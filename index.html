One platform, different moments

From collaborative discovery to individual demonstration.

Build POGIL-style activities where small groups investigate, discuss, and construct answers together. The platform can assign roles, coordinate whose turn it is, and keep the group’s shared work visible.

  • Shared group workspace
  • Facilitator, analyst, quality-check, and spokesperson roles
  • Turn rotation and section timers
  • Text, Python, and C++ activity components

Deliver individual assessments in a dedicated test mode. Instructors can set a start time and duration, choose students, randomize multiple-choice options, and use a single final submission with automatic grading.

  • Individual assessment instances
  • Scheduled start and timed window
  • Optional question-order randomization
  • No live feedback during the test

Give every student a personal workspace for homework or lab work, or configure a collaborative experience. Due dates, reopening controls, completion status, and score summaries help keep the work manageable.

  • Individual or collaborative assignment setup
  • Per-student and bulk due-date controls
  • Completion, in-progress, and score views
  • Graded activities for homework and labs

Let prospective users experience coLearn-AI without class setup. Demo paths offer student, instructor, and creator perspectives—useful for a first look, training, and conversations about pedagogy.

  • Student, instructor, and creator demo paths
  • Join a live group or explore solo
  • Safe space to see classroom workflows
  • Activity preview and revision loop
